The continent of Australia exhibits seasonal snowfall within high-altitude sub-alpine regions, a phenomenon frequently counter to baseline geographical expectations. Initial exposure to this empirical anomaly induces a psychological response characterized by cognitive dissonance, novel perception, and perceptual astonishment. The primary objective of this project is to systematically evoke this precise affective state within the observer through deliberate creative inquiry, prompting the question of what specific cognitive or emotional response such an anomaly elicits.
